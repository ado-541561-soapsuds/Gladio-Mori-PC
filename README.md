## 🕹️ About

Gladio Mori is a physics-driven gladiator fighting game set in brutal arena battles where every strike can decide the outcome. Developed by Plebeian Studio and published by Bonus Stage Publishing, it replaces traditional health bars with localized injuries, organ damage, severed arteries, and limb loss. Fight through a solo campaign, create custom movesets, customize your gladiator, and compete in local or cross-platform multiplayer battles for up to four players. Its focus on momentum, timing, anatomy, and emergent physics makes every duel unpredictable and intensely tactical.

Gladio Mori is for players who enjoy technical fighting games, brutal swordplay, physics-based combat, and competitive arena battles.

## ✨ Features

- 🩸 **Localized Damage** — Target muscles, arteries, vital organs, and limbs instead of relying on health bars.
- ⚔️ **Physics-Driven Combat** — Use timing, momentum, positioning, and precision to control every lethal exchange.
- 🎭 **Custom Movesets** — Build distinctive fighting styles by designing and combining personalized attacks.
- 👥 **Multiplayer Battles** — Compete in local and online matches supporting up to four players with cross-play.
- 📽️ **Combat Replays** — Save memorable fights and review them from multiple camera angles.
- 🏟️ **Gladiator Arenas** — Battle across dangerous arenas where movement and environmental awareness matter.
